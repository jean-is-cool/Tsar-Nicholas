Tsar Nicholas III

Who is Tsar Nicholas III? It's a question many have asked, and to which there is no single answer. But this webpage aims to explain the life and impacts of one of the most extraordinary men in modern times.


Life

The exact date of Tsar Nicholas's birth is unknown. However, it has been estimated by experts to have been sometime in the year 2026. Tsar Nicholas was born into the clutches of an evil company who sell people as food. This company sold him to another company, who had a shop in Hobart.

Despite being trapped in a tiny box along with almost one hundred of his fellows, without nutritious food to eat, and in a refrigerator at only one or two degrees above zero, the Tsar spent his early childhood studying and becoming knowledgeable about the world. Because of his learning, he knew that his destiny was to be eaten before ever reaching adulthood.

But one day, when the Tsar was only about seven instars old, his box was bought. It was bought by Jean. She took him home, and whilst she did feed some of his siblings to her lizard, she was better to them than the pet shop had been: she let them live outside of a refrigerator. The warm air allowed the Tsar to grow quickly and pupate before he could be eaten.

The Tsar, on reaching adulthood, was put into a new box. This box contained very nutritious food, such as apple cores and oats. It also contained a lot of ladies (his sisters, but we try not to think about that). The Tsar had some fun with these ladies, and all the while his brain grew bigger and bigger because of the nutritious food.

And one day (25 September 2026), the Tsar's brain grew so big that he realised there was more to life than mating with his sisters. So he opened his elytra, unfurled his wings, and flew up out of the box. He landed on Jean's blazer.

He stayed on Jean's blazer as she walked to school. When they arrived, he jumped off to hide in her locker. But she saw him, and kept him with her all day, showing him the world. He met many people, and these people became his devoted fans. It was for them that this website was created.

The Tsar now lives happily in his own special box, with one or two of his sisters present at all times. He is currently planning his next adventure at school.

Species

The Tsar, as most know, is not human. He is a beetle of the species Tenebrio molitor. Here is some more information about that very interesting kind of animal.

Tenebrio molitor larvae are called mealworms. They are used as feeder animals for birds, reptiles, fish, and sometimes small mammals such as mice. A recent development is the promotion of mealworms, especially mealworm flour (which is ground-up dried mealworms), as food for humans.

Tenebrio molitor are sometimes called yellow mealworm beetles. This can be misleading because while the larvae are yellow, the adults are black. However, right after pupation they are cream-coloured, then turn red, then black. This is the process of melanisation. Sclerotisation also occurs, which is the hardening of the exoskeleton. It has been observed that the exoskeleton of the Tsar, when seen in the right light, has beautiful undertones of red.

As adults, Tenebrio molitor are only supposed to live for two or three months. :(
