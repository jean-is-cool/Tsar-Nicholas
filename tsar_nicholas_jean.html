About Jean

Jean is the creator of this website. The Tsar, although at an intellectually genius level for his species, is unable to build a website. So, upon hearing that there was great demand for a website devoted to him, he commissioned Jean to make it.

Jean also handles the Tsar's e-mail correspondence, as he is too small to type on a standard keyboard. However, fans can be assured that every e-mail with his signature at the end was dictated in its entirety by the Tsar himself.


'But who is Jean really?' you ask. Well, she's a person. At the time of writing this sentence, she's fifteen. She lives in Hobart. She enjoys making websites. But this website isn't about her. So stop reading about Jean, the boring web developer, and start reading about that infinitely amazing and interesting man, Tsar Nicholas.
