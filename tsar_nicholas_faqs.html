Frequently asked questions

Whilst the Tsar greatly enjoys replying to fan mail, there are some questions that he gets tired of answering because of how often they come up. So, before writing to ask the Tsar a question, check this page. You may well find an answer.

(If any of this information seems familiar, that's probably because it was mentioned in the About the Tsar page. But not everyone cares as much about the wonderful Tsar as you obviously do, and might not be willing to read all of that for an answer. That is the reason for the creation of this page.)


What species is Tsar Nicholas?

Tsar Nicholas is not human. He is a beetle of the species Tenebrio molitor. However, he does not let this stop him from being one of the most extraordinary men alive. And for his species, he has a genius-level IQ.

Tenebrio molitor are small, black beetles. Their larvae are commonly called mealworms. The Tsar started his life as a mealworm, progressed to a pupa, and finally pupated to become the wonderful beetle he is today.

How did Tsar Nicholas become famous?

The Tsar used to be one of approximately one hundred of his kind, all living together in a plastic box, feasting on oats and apple core. He was, for a while, content in this boring life.

But one day, the Tsar realised he was meant for something more. He decided to see the world. So he sneaked onto Jean's blazer overnight. In the morning, she went to school with the Tsar still on it, unknown to her. On arrival at school, Jean noticed the Tsar, but it was too late to take him back. He would have to stay the day at school.

During this time, the Tsar met many people. These people became the Tsar's devoted fans, and demanded that a website be made in his honour. That is how the Tsar became famous.

When is Tsar Nicholas Day?

The National Day in Honour of Tsar Nicholas III (often shortened to Tsar Nicholas Day) is 25 September. There are days (rounded down) until the next Tsar Nicholas Day.

25 September is not the Tsar's birthday. Rather, it is the date of his first adventure, when he became famous and saw the world.

Will Tsar Nicholas visit school again?

The Tsar enjoyed highly his first visit to school on 25 September 2026. However, he is a delicate man who needs time to rest. He will not come to school every day.

This does not mean that the Tsar will never come back. He has definite plans to return sometime in the near future. And if he gets too tired, perhaps one of his many cousins will come along instead.

How often is the newsletter sent?

The Tsar's newsletter is sent out by e-mail every second Saturday.

I have something to say about Tsar Nicholas; can I contribute to the website?

Definitely! Jean often finds herself running out of ideas of what to write about the Tsar. If you have information, fun facts, or just want to share your opinion on the wonderful Tsar, please e-mail your writing to the Tsar with 'Webpage' in the subject line and a new webpage will be added with your information.

Of course not! The Tsar owns himself. In fact, it was he who commissioned her to create this website.
